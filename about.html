Extending the Food Basket to the Nations

BENUE FOOD GLOBAL is an agricultural food distribution company headquartered in Benue State. We connect buyers directly with local farmers in Benue, reducing middlemen so households, individuals, and bulk buyers get direct access to fresh Benue food products.

Browse Our Products

Our Promise: Farm-Fresh Nutrition, Zero Middleman Friction

We are committed to connecting the richness of Benue’s fertile agricultural land with every Nigerian home and partner abroad. Through strong, direct partnerships with trusted local farmers, we sort, clean, and package authentic staples ready for your kitchen.

  • Head Office: Benue State, Nigeria — coordinating harvest aggregation, sorting & packaging.
  • Pickup Stations: Lagos & Abuja — fast local collection for households and businesses.
  • Core Staples: Nigerian Local Rice, Pure Honey, Beans, Garri, Yam Flour, and Palm Oil.
