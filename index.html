Why we created Benue Food Global

To bring farm-fresh Benue food directly to households, individuals, and wholesale buyers without middleman friction:

  • Direct partnerships with trusted local farmers in Benue State.
  • Stone-free, sorted Nigerian Local Rice in 5kg and 10kg sealed packs.
  • 100% pure, unadulterated Benue Honey and rich natural Palm Oil.
  • Cleanly processed Beans (5kg), Crispy Garri (2kg), and Yam Flour (1kg).
  • Hygienic sorting and moisture-locked food-grade packaging.
  • Head Office in Benue State with Lagos & Abuja pickup stations.
  • Order online with instant request or via WhatsApp on 08083690567.

Wholesale, Distribution & Strategic Partnerships

Beyond individual household baskets, Benue Food Global supports commercial buyers, cooperatives, logistics partners, and agricultural investors across all five business lines:

  • Farmer & Supplier Partnerships: Fair off-take for Benue growers & apiaries.
