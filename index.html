Connecting Nigerian Producers to Key Food Markets

BENUE FOOD GLOBAL (BFG) is a Nigerian agricultural sourcing, aggregation, packaging and food distribution company headquartered in Benue State, Nigeria. We are focused on connecting farmers and trusted agricultural suppliers with households, retailers, wholesalers, restaurants, food vendors, institutions and other food buyers.

With Benue State as an important sourcing base and a focus on markets including Abuja and Lagos, BFG is building a distribution network designed to connect agricultural supply with demand across Nigeria. Our long-term ambition is to grow into a trusted food sourcing and distribution company with wider national reach.

Our 8-Step Sourcing & Distribution Process

BFG works across key stages of the food supply chain to coordinate quality-focused handling and dependable communication:

  • 1. Source identification: Identify suitable farmers, markets and trusted suppliers.
  • 2. Supplier engagement: Discuss availability, product details, quantity and supply terms.
  • 3. Aggregation: Assemble produce in quantities suitable for customer demand.
  • 4. Quality checks: Check condition and suitability against agreed requirements.
  • 5. Packaging & preparation: Prepare and pack products as appropriate.
  • 6. Order coordination: Confirm customer orders and delivery arrangements.
  • 7. Distribution & 8. Customer support: Dispatch through agreed logistics and handle feedback.

LEADERSHIP

Victor Eje Agama — Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Victor Eje Agama is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of BENUE FOOD GLOBAL (BFG), a Nigerian agribusiness focused on agricultural sourcing, aggregation, packaging and food distribution. He leads the company’s strategic direction and its efforts to build connections between agricultural producers, suppliers and food buyers.

Under his leadership, BFG is working to develop a structured sourcing and distribution model that supports access to Nigerian food products, strengthens relationships across the agricultural supply chain and serves customers and business buyers in key markets. His vision is to build BENUE FOOD GLOBAL into a trusted and scalable food sourcing and distribution company.

“At BENUE FOOD GLOBAL, we believe that connecting agricultural producers to the right markets is an important part of building a stronger food supply chain. Our commitment is to develop dependable sourcing relationships, serve our customers with care and build a business that contributes to the growth of Nigeria’s agricultural and food distribution sector. We invite farmers, suppliers, customers and strategic partners to grow with us.”
